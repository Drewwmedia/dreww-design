Density

Controls are 40 px tall by default and 32 px in toolbars and tables. Table rows are 52 px. Panels pad 24 px, the page pads 32 px, gaps between panels are 20 or 24 px.

No odd values. When a gap feels tight, go up a step on the scale, never down. The app should breathe like the website, just with smaller type.

Cut corner · top-left always square

xs · 4

Tags, checkboxes, keyboard keys, avatars on brand tiles.

sm · 8

Buttons, inputs, selects, nav items, notices.

md · 12

Panels, KPI cards, large buttons. Same as the website button.

lg · 16

Modals, drawers and the app frame. Same as website cards.

Elevation and motion

One shadow, for floating layers only

Menus, popovers, modals and toasts. Cards and panels sit flat with a Grey 200 border, like on the website.

A menu, floating

Two speeds

120 ms for tints: row hover, tabs, inputs. 350 ms with the wipe curve for buttons, the website's 0.5 s shortened for a tool people click all day.

No bounce. Motion is turned off for anyone who asks for reduced motion.

Notices & toasts

Notices stay on the page until the problem is fixed. Toasts confirm something just happened, then leave. Anything someone has to act on is a notice, never a toast.

Notices

White, Grey 200 border, a coloured icon. Bold first sentence says what happened, the rest says what it means and links the fix.

Chart rules

SeriesBurgundy, coral, blue, gold, in that order. Four at most. A fifth folds into Other, or the chart splits into small multiples.
Colour follows the thingMeta is always burgundy, Google always coral. A filter never repaints what's left.
ComparisonPrevious period and benchmarks are a Grey 500 line, never a series colour.
Good and badTeal and red stay on deltas and status. They never colour a series.
One axisNever two y-axes. Two measures with different units get two charts.
MarksLines 2 px. Bars 24 px thick at most, 4 px rounded end, square at the baseline. 2 px white gap between stacked parts. Area fill at 10%.
Grid and axesGrey 200 hairlines, solid. Axis text DM Sans 12 in Grey 600. Round tick values.
LabelsText is ink or grey, never the series colour. Label the end or the one value the story is about, not every point.
So farData that's still maturing is dashed and named in the legend.
Every chart hasAn Anton title, a subtitle with the period and unit, a legend for two or more series, a hover tooltip, a table view and a source line.

Date range picker

On every Reporting page. Presets on the left for the usual ranges, a two-month calendar for anything else, and the comparison period set in the same place. Try it: pick a preset, or click two days.

Picker

Opens from the trigger in the page toolbar. The trigger shows the range in ink and the comparison in grey, so you always know what a delta is measured against.

Range endsBurgundy, cut corner

Rules

DefaultLast 30 days, ending yesterday. Today is never in a preset because its data is still coming in.
FormatOct 26 – Nov 24, 2026. The year shows once, unless the range crosses into a new year.
ComparisonPrevious period of the same length by default, or the same period last year. It's what every delta on the page measures against.
Time zoneDates follow the client's ad account time zone. The footer says so when it differs from yours.
Short versionA toolbar with room for only a few ranges uses the segmented control (7D, 30D, 90D) with Custom opening this picker.
CalendarWeeks start on Monday. Future days can't be picked. The range stays the same as you move between a client's pages.

LayoutOne column of fields, 520 px at most. Section title and context on the left, fields on the right. Stacks on narrow screens.
