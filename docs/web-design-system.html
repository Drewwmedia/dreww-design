Logo

Wordmark by default. Monogram for avatars and small spaces.

Monogram

Do

  • Black or burgundy on light backgrounds.
  • White or coral on burgundy and dark backgrounds.
  • Keep the ® attached to the wordmark.
  • Use the files exactly as provided.

Don't

  • Stretch, squash, rotate or redraw it.
  • Use any colour beyond black, white, burgundy and coral.
  • Add gradients, shadows or outlines.
  • Place it on a busy background.

Where the header and footer disagree

Corners

The header Book a call button is 8 px. The tab fill and search field are 12 px, which matches the agreed buttons.

12 px on the header button too.

Footer labels

Two eyebrow styles in one footer: coral at 400 weight (Montreal HQ, Platforms) and faded white at 500 (the link columns), with different spacing.

One style: Plex Mono 500, 0.1em. Coral for the first label only, faded white for the columns.

Naming

The footer says "App Growth & User Acquisition" and "Book a Call". Everywhere else it's "User Acquisition" and "Book a call".

Match the rest of the site.

Decisions

What we agreed, and what still has to change on the site and the brand kit.

Final CTA: ink with the warm glow

Keep it as live. The brand kit's "burgundy for CTA bands" rule gets updated.

To do: update /brand-kit and brand.md

Buttons: the home hero button is the standard

Coral, 12 px cut corner, Anton 20 px, white wipe on hover. Shown in Buttons & links, next.

To do: Final CTA button 14 → 12 px · brand kit 8 → 12 px

Hover: left-to-right wipe

Same on primary and ghost. Alternatives explored and set aside. No darker shades, grow or lift.

To do: remove #702242, #C95D4F and scale(1.03) from the theme

Eyebrows: 0.1em spacing

A little tighter than the brand kit's 0.12em.

To do: homepage eyebrows (now 0) · brand kit 0.12 → 0.1em

H2: 54 px, 62 px on large monitors

One fluid size instead of two fixed ones.

To do: theme H2 token

Teal #215F6A for positive states

UI only, never a brand colour. Replaces the site's green #2F7D4F.

To do: theme success token · add to brand kit as UI-only

Gradients: the live glows

Four glows: Final CTA, footer, open menu and behind media. All dark. No flat burgundy-to-coral gradient.

To do: remove --gradient-primary and --gradient-secondary from the theme

Coral buttons keep white text

Accepted exception: 2.9:1 contrast, below AA. Kept for brand recognition.

Cards: cut corners, two hovers, one border

Service card stays as built today, with the cut corner (alternatives explored and set aside). 16 px cut corner for big cards, 12 px for small. Ink spread for action cards, coral border for reading cards. No lift. Grey 200 borders.

To do: service, FAQ and review cards to cut corners · drop case card lift · swap border colours

8 px cut corner on inputs, 12 px on option cards. Focus: white, burgundy border, coral ring. Errors in red #DA1E28 with an icon, the same as the apps (agreed Nov 25).
