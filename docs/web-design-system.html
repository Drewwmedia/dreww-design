Logo

Wordmark by default. Monogram for avatars and small spaces.

Monogram

Do

  • Black or burgundy on light backgrounds.
  • White or coral on burgundy and dark backgrounds.
  • Keep the ® attached to the wordmark.
  • Use the files exactly as provided.

Don't

  • Stretch, squash, rotate or redraw it.
  • Use any colour beyond black, white, burgundy and coral.
  • Add gradients, shadows or outlines.
  • Place it on a busy background.
